We complement your editor or production company

We come in as extra capacity for AI, special scenes, VFX or workload peaks, without replacing your team or the vendors you already produce your campaigns with.

Direction, not just clip generation

If your team already uses AI tools, we add what's usually missing: cinematic direction, character consistency, scene integration, sound and deliverable control from start to finish.

First, we decide when AI makes sense

AI isn't always cheaper. Before quoting, we review scenes, revisions and rights, and tell you whether AI, a live shoot or hybrid production makes sense for that specific idea.

Fidelity limits agreed in writing

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How do you quote?+

Per project or through a monthly retainer, based on scenes, length, versions, revisions and rights. Always in writing before production starts.

Do you work white label?+

Yes, with an NDA and explicit commercial terms.

Who owns the rights to the pieces?+

Defined in the contract: licenses, commercial use, likeness, characters, music and voice.

What's the turnaround time with the external cell?+

Whatever is set in the contract based on priority and volume.

Do you also shoot on set?+

Yes. When the piece calls for it, we shoot with a professional crew.

Do you guarantee paid media results?+

No. We design every piece for its goal and give you what you need to measure it, but results also depend on media, distribution and the campaign's creative.

Can you support us on a pitch?+

Yes, with a visual exploration or proof of concept of agreed scope.
