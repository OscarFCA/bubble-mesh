What owners ask us

We record without interrupting the night

We work with a small, discreet crew, coordinated with your floor manager, to capture the real atmosphere without getting in your servers' way or bothering your guests at any point.

Band permissions cleared in advance

Before each date we confirm with the artists what can be recorded and posted, so your lineup grows without conflicts with the bands or the rights to their music.

Trackable reservations even when your audience comes in directly

We combine booking links, per-post codes, WhatsApp messages and a simple question at the door, so you know where your guests came from even if they don't book online.

We work alongside your current community manager

If someone already runs your social media, we hand them produced, ready-to-post content and take care of measurement and your lineup page.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How many nights a month do you shoot?+

We set it after the pilot based on your calendar. The usual approach is to cover the key dates and promote all of them.

Do I need a reservation system?+

No. We connect the one you already use or set up a simple one with WhatsApp and a form.

Do you build the event listings page?+

Yes, with dates, photos, showtimes and a booking button. We can also add it to your current website.

How do I pay?+

The pilot is paid upfront and the monthly plan at the start of each month.
