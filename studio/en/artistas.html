How it works

Your 90-day pilot, in stages

  1. 1

    Weeks 1 and 2: diagnosis and groundwork

    We audit your accounts, define your identity and bio, review permissions on past material and set the metrics to track.

  2. 2

Before you ask

You start with what you already have: your shows

You don't need an expensive music video to get started. Your live shows are already your best material; we record them well and turn them into weeks of content that works for you.

Your past work, used with permission

Before posting, we review which material from past eras you can use and which you can't, so your new chapter grows without name, image or repertoire conflicts with anyone.

We measure what we can actually control

We don't promise magic followers. We agree on deliverables and metrics from day one, and every month you see which format brought in real followers, visits and booking requests.

AI helps, your voice leads

We use AI for visuals and scenes a camera can't reach, never to replace your voice or your image, and always with your written approval before any piece is published.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the pilot cost?+

It depends on how many events we cover and how many pieces you need. After the assessment we give you a fixed quote in writing.

Do you shoot outside Mexico City?+

Yes, we shoot wherever you have a date. Crew travel expenses are itemized in the quote.

Do you post on my social media?+

We can publish for you or hand everything over ready for your team. We decide that in the assessment.

Can you make the music video for my single?+
