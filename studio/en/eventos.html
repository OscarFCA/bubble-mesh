We work with your current vendors

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?+

Ideally three weeks or more ahead; streaming and the closing recap require a site visit.

Do you cover events outside Mexico City?+

Yes. Crew travel expenses are itemized in the quote.

Can you stream a concert with music on YouTube?+

Yes, but first we review licenses and rights management, since the platform can cut off a stream that includes protected material.

What does the sponsor report include?+

List of pieces delivered per brand, screenshots and links, plus real metrics when available, kept separate from any estimate.

How soon do you deliver the aftermovie?+

Turnaround is set in the quote based on the event's length and the versions you need.

Can you fly a drone at my venue?+

It depends on the space, safety and permits. We confirm it during the technical site visit.
