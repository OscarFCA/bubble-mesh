Scope and considerations+
  • 24-hour audio streaming and per-event video are two different kinds of load. Cloudflare Stream is reserved mainly for video; there's no need to send still-image video to run the radio signal.
  • Automatic recording may require source setup, start and end scheduling per broadcast, and external automations. Don't promise that every broadcast turns into an edited, published episode on its own.
  • Video costs depend on minutes stored and played; audio costs depend on concurrent listeners, bandwidth and provider plan.
  • Indefinite archive storage is not included: retention, backup, rights and capacity policies need to be defined.
  • Design and software don't include cameras, mixing console, switcher, microphones, redundant connectivity or human broadcast operation, unless specifically contracted.
  • Building this platform is an implementation project, with recurring support and infrastructure. It's separate from the monthly content editing and distribution plan.
Metrics

Live listeners, video plays, episodes consumed, watch time, returning users, signal uptime, published library and conversions on sponsor content (when measurable).

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Do we need cameras in the booth?+

Not to start. We work with the audio and video you already record; visual radio is quoted separately.

Can we stream the radio live from our website?+

Yes. We integrate your current streaming URL if it's compatible, or set up a suitable digital audio source.

Do you also stream live video?+

Yes, as an add-on module from the studio and a video vendor. Equipment and directing are budgeted separately.

Are the episodes archived?+

We design a recording, review and publishing workflow so they're available in an organized library, subject to rights and storage policy.

Do you publish on Spotify, YouTube, Apple Podcasts and other platforms?+

We can prepare and manage posts on platforms that support the client's format and account. We check the requirements of each destination.

How many clips come out of each episode?+

Between 5 and 10, depending on the length and content of the show. We fine-tune it during the pilot.

Do you publish, or does our team?+

Your call: we can schedule posts on your accounts with your approval, or hand you everything ready to go.

How is it billed?+

Monthly or by episode volume. The pilot is quoted separately and in writing.

Do you work with radio stations outside Mexico City?+

Yes. Editing is done remotely; on-site capture adds travel expenses.

What about the music in the clips?+

We review it before publishing and, if needed, adjust the clip to avoid copyright blocks.
