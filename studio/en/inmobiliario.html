Your current renders become video

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you work from renders I already have?+

Yes. We turn them into video, a walkthrough or social content, and add new scenes if needed.

What is a 360° tour with LIDAR?+

A scan of the space that lets your prospect walk through the property virtually from their phone or computer.

Can you fly a drone anywhere?+

No. It depends on location, safety and permits. We confirm before scheduling.

Do you build the development's landing page?+

Yes, with a gallery, tour, location and a form connected to your CRM or your sales WhatsApp.

How can you tell an image was made with AI?+

With a visible “illustrative image” label. We recommend your legal team review the copy before publishing.

Do you work outside Mexico City?+

Yes. Crew travel expenses are itemized in the quote.
