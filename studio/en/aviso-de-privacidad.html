Legal

Full privacy notice

In accordance with the current Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties. Last updated: 10/26/2026.

1. Data controller

Vinculando Humanos con Servicios Integrales de Inteligencia, S.A.P.I. de C.V. (RFC VHS2407264EA), which operates the BubbleMesh Studio brand, based in Mexico City, is responsible for processing your personal data. Privacy contact: contacto@bubble-mesh.com.

2. Personal data we collect

  • Identification and contact data: name, WhatsApp or phone number and, if you choose, email address.
  • Request details: project type, estimated timeline and any information you choose to share.
  • Browsing data: technical information collected through cookies and similar technologies (see section 8).
  • WhatsApp conversations: your number, your profile name and the content of the messages you send us.

We don't request sensitive personal data. Please don't include it in your messages.

3. Purposes

Necessary purposes (establish the relationship with you):

  • Respond to your request and contact you through the channel you chose.
  • Prepare and send you quotes and proposals.
  • Follow up on your project and, where applicable, enter into and carry out the services agreement.
  • Invoice the services you hire.

Purposes that require your consent (not required to handle your request):

  • Send you information about the studio's services, case studies and news.
  • Invite you to take satisfaction surveys.
  • Measure the performance of our ad campaigns.

If you don't want us to process your data for these purposes, you can tell us at any time by writing to contacto@bubble-mesh.com. Declining does not affect how we handle your request.

4. WhatsApp virtual assistant and use of artificial intelligence

When you message us on WhatsApp, a virtual assistant may reply automatically to give you information, provide a quote or book an appointment. The assistant identifies itself as such, and you can ask to speak with a person at any time by writing “talk to a person”. Automated replies do not make decisions that affect you definitively; any commercial proposal is reviewed by a member of our team.

To run the assistant, we use messaging providers (WhatsApp, by Meta) and artificial intelligence providers that process messages on our behalf.

5. Who we share your data with

To provide the contracted service, we may share the necessary data with the technical production providers involved in your project. We also use providers that process data on our behalf (website hosting, forms, lead sheet or CRM, email and messaging), who may only use it to provide that service to us.

We don't sell your personal data.

6. ARCO rights

You can access, correct or cancel your data, or object to its processing (ARCO rights), by sending a request to contacto@bubble-mesh.com with:

  • Your name and a way to reach you.
  • A copy of an official ID or, where applicable, documents proving legal representation.
  • A clear description of the data and the right you want to exercise.
  • Any document that helps us locate your data.

We'll respond within the timeframe set by law.

7. Withdrawal of consent and limits on use

You can revoke your consent using the same procedure described in section 6, or stop receiving communications by replying “STOP” to any promotional message. In some cases, we may not be able to fulfill your request immediately if a legal or contractual obligation requires us to retain certain data.

8. Cookies and tracking technologies

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to measure visits and site usage (Google Analytics 4) and ad effectiveness (Meta pixel and Google Ads conversion tag). These tools may collect data such as IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, time on site and the source of the visit. They are only activated if you accept them in the cookie notice; you can reject them or disable them in your browser settings.

9. Retention

We keep your data for as long as needed to handle your request and, if you hire us, for the duration of the relationship and the applicable legal periods.

10. Changes to this notice

Any changes to this notice will be posted on this same page, with the date of the update.

11. Authority

If you believe your right to personal data protection has been violated, you may contact the Ministry of Anti-Corruption and Good Governance (Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno).
