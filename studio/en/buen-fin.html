Frequently asked questions

Is it really ready in 5 business days?+

Yes, counted from the deposit and once we have all your information: offer, logo and product photos if you have them.

Do I have to be registered for El Buen Fin?+

Not to advertise your offers, but you do need it to use the official logo. Registration is free until November 12, and we help you with it.

How much should I invest in ad spend?+

We recommend from MXN 10,000 for the five days and the week before. With less, the campaign doesn't have enough to learn.

Do you guarantee sales?+

No. We guarantee the commercial, a well-built campaign and a clear report. Sales also depend on your offer, your price and your inventory.

Can I use the commercial after Buen Fin?+

Yes. With the Christmas and Three Kings extension, we update it for the season.

What if there are no spots left?+

We'll let you know first about the Christmas and Three Kings package.
