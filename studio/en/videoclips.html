How we make your music video with you

1

We listen to the track and build the concept

2

Script, storyboard and shooting plan

Before you ask

AI helps, your voice leads

We use AI for settings and scenes a camera can't reach, never to replace your voice or your image, and always with your written approval before any piece is published.

A music video for every budget

A lyric video, a visualizer or a live session cost very differently than a story-driven music video. We'll suggest the format that best tells your song's story within your budget.

Ready for your release date

In the proposal we agree on the delivery date, teaser and social media versions, so your launch arrives complete, not with loose pieces posted days after the premiere.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a music video cost?+

It depends on the type, locations and special scenes. We'll send you a written proposal after listening to the track.

How long does it take?+

From script approval; the schedule is set in the proposal based on the type of music video.

Do I need the song to be finished?+

To shoot, we do need the final version or something very close; to plan the concept, a demo is enough.

Who owns the music video?+

Yours to promote your music; the contract spells out usage rights, music and images.

Do you use AI in every music video?+

No. Only when your song calls for it and with your permission.

Do you work outside Mexico City?+

Yes. The team's travel expenses are itemized in the proposal.
