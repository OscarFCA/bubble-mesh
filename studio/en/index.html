Script · Storyboard · Character sheets

From script to screen

Before we generate or shoot anything, we write the script, draw the storyboard and define every character with a reference sheet. That way an AI and VFX piece keeps the same face, the same light and the same story from start to finish. This is the real process behind our Teatro Nacional piece.

01Script

We write the scene: place, era, characters and what the viewer should feel.

02Storyboard

We draw every shot before production, so framing and pacing get approved without spending a single shoot day.

03Character sheets

Every character gets a reference sheet, so it keeps the same face in every scene.

04Keyframe

We lock the visual world: the light, the era and the architecture of every location.

Final piece · Teatro Nacional
05Final piece

Once everything is approved, we generate, animate and edit the piece with sound.

That's why an AI video can look like your brand: it's directed before it's generated.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?+

An AI commercial starts at MXN 8,900, and a website with a WhatsApp that answers starts at MXN 14,900 plus MXN 2,900 a month. Every project is quoted in writing before we start.

How long does it take?+

An AI commercial is delivered in 5 business days, and a website with its WhatsApp assistant in 10 business days, counted from when we receive your information and the deposit.

Who owns a video made with AI?+

You can use the delivered pieces to promote your business with no time limit. The contract spells out usage rights, music and images.

How many changes are included?+

One round of changes on the commercial and two on the website. Additional changes are quoted before we make them.

What do you need from me?+

Your logo, your products or services, your prices and the questions your customers ask most. If you have photos or videos, those help too.

Does AI replace filming?+

Not always. Sometimes it's better to film, sometimes to create with AI, and often to combine both. We tell you which works best before we quote.

Does the WhatsApp assistant answer on its own?+

Yes, it answers questions, quotes and books appointments at any hour, and hands the conversation to you when a person is needed. You decide what it can answer.

Do you issue invoices?+

Yes, we issue an invoice for every service.
