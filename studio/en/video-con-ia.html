What is an AI commercial or advertising spot?

A commercial, or advertising spot, is a short video, usually 15 to 60 seconds long, made to sell or promote a product, a service or a brand. It runs on social media, YouTube, Google Ads, TV or screens at points of sale and events.

In an AI commercial, scenes aren't filmed with cameras: they are generated with artificial intelligence models from a script and real photos and videos of your brand. The result is edited like any commercial, with voice-over, music, subtitles and your logo.

The difference is what you no longer need: locations, permits, actors, wardrobe or shoot days. That's why it takes days instead of weeks, and it allows scenes that would be impossible or very expensive to film.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an AI commercial cost?+

From MXN 8,900 + VAT for a commercial up to 30 seconds with a 15-second version. For ongoing content, the package of 4 videos, 5 clips and 24 ad designs costs MXN 14,569 + VAT.

Where does the price of an AI commercial come from?+

From everything you don't see. We handle the tedious part: choosing the right video model for each scene, generating and curating images and videos until they look right, creating the AI voice-over and blending it with the music and edit. You get the finished commercial, without learning tools or paying for subscriptions.

What's the difference between the express video and the spot?+

The express is a single scene generated straight from your material, ideal for content. The spot goes through script, storyboard, character sheets and keyframes, and includes your voice-over: it's the format for paid ads.

How long does it take?+

Five business days from when you approve the script.

What do I need to send?+

Photos and videos of your product, your logo, the offer you want to communicate and your brand's tone.

Can I run it on Meta, TikTok, YouTube and Google?+

Yes. We deliver it in vertical and horizontal, with a license for social media and digital ads.

Can you tell it's made with AI?+

It depends on the style you choose, from realistic to animated. Some platforms require AI-made content to be labeled; we tell you when that applies to your ad.

Do you work white-label for agencies?+

Yes. No logo of ours, a signed confidentiality agreement and deliveries under your agency's name.

Do you also make commercials with real people?+

Yes. As a production company we film on location, cover events and produce corporate videos.
