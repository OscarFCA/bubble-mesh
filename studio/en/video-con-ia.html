What people usually ask us

Looks like your brand, not a template

We work with a script, storyboard and character sheets, so every scene keeps the same face, the same light and the same story from start to finish, in your brand colors.

You approve the story before we produce

Before we generate or shoot, we show you the script and storyboard. If something doesn't feel right, we fix it on paper, which is much faster and cheaper than redoing scenes.

Sometimes it's better to shoot, and we'll tell you

Not everything turns out better or cheaper with AI. Before we quote, we tell you what's best to film, what to create with AI and what to combine, based on the quality and control you need.

No one appears without their permission

We don't create or alter a real person's face or voice without their written permission, and the contract clearly defines the usage rights for each piece.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an AI commercial cost?+

How long does it take?+

Who owns the video?+

You can use it to promote your business with no time limit. The contract spells out music, images and usage rights.
